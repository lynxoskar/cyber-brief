ConnectWise Cloud Backup compromise: Huntress counts unauthorized sign-ins in 162 Microsoft 365 tenants

A stolen Cloud Backup credential was used against customer tenants; Microsoft says attackers likely abused permissions customers had granted the app, including Directory.ReadWrite.All. Huntress also saw a second, mostly unsuccessful wave aimed at policy, national security, trade and critical-minerals targets. No restoration timeline; ConnectWise says it cannot see whether a given tenant was breached.

Krebs, citing sources close to the probe: Signal chats seized from arrested suspect Pepijn van der Stap show executives at two negotiation firms knew of victims early, and arrested Canadian negotiator Edward Dubrovsky allegedly knew of the FBI data theft without telling the bureau. VectorsNew attack vectors & techniques

  • AOMEI trick: attackers edited two promo/notification config files on the vendor servers only briefly, then restored them, so the app fetched a malicious ZIP and ran it via the notification feature with no user click. Hard to catch without continuous monitoring. BleepingComputer
  • GhostAction adds a second stage borrowed from North Korea's PolinRider: a committed tasks.json plus fake fa-solid-400.woff2 makes VS Code autorun a stealer and install a cron job; 94 repos found. OpenSourceMalware
  • iOS exploit kits on compromised sites: Unit 42 flags domains tied to Coruna-like and DarkSword activity that tell visitors to open pages in Safari on iPhone; MalwareHunterTeam says there are too many compromised sites to list (vx-underground cites 183 found by @AelsMartin, unconfirmed). Unit 42 on X
  • "WPSplinter": Broadcom WPS fragment-reassembly bug gives in-range attackers root on Broadcom-based routers without the Wi-Fi password; no CVE or fix yet, older eCos chips likely never patched. Demoed on Proximus, Orange Belgium and VOO gateways. Quentin Kaiser

AI/CloudAI & cloud security

  • Wiz CIRT: in several recent intrusions an AI agent system ran the whole operation, finding entry points, harvesting credentials, pivoting across cloud, Kubernetes, CI/CD and identity, and debugging its own scripts. Loud and fast, but fragmented across logs. Wiz
  • MITRE ATT&CK v20: prompt injection split into direct and indirect techniques, plus abuse of an organization's own AI agents for data theft, AI search poisoning, app-specific password theft to dodge MFA, and coding-agent config files under supply chain. SecurityWeek

Elastic Security Labs
