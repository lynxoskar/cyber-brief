VectorsNew attack vectors & techniques

  • SaaS backup app as pivot: stolen app secret plus pre-granted Entra permissions gives tenant-wide read/write with no user login, sidestepping MFA (ConnectWise case). BleepingComputer
  • AOMEI Backupper update servers tampered with in 5 to 10 minute windows (May to July) to push a backdoor; unsigned update config files made it possible. Disclosed by GTIG, Mandiant and ManageEngine. The Hacker News
  • MALFEX npm package "imgdecode" hides a shellcode loader in a native Windows addon (979 downloads, still live at time of report). The Hacker News
  • OpenBSD 7.9 root exploit chains a ~37-year-old shmat integer overflow with a UAF, bypassing KARL/SMAP/SMEP; patched in September errata, PoC public. The Hacker News
